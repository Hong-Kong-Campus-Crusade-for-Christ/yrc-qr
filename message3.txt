回程+Dinner： 唔用手機 🚫📱

反思：滑手機使大腦持續接收和處理大量瑣碎資訊，無法真正休息，進而導致心靈過勞。頻繁的手機通知使生活與工作界線模糊，打斷專注狀態，並難以活在當下。這個操練旨在透過限制使用手機，讓你反思：<b>究竟你與手機，誰是擁有者？當手機成為了「軛」- 心時刻被它所牽動、刺激、吸引，你將如何面對手機對心帶來的「軛」？</b>

回程+Dinner：每次跟人說話之前，要先表達內心感受。

反思：你過去最近一次時刻觸摸內心，並表達自己感受是什麼時候? 很多時間我們表達背後往往帶著很多感受，有時更時同時出現多個感受，表達前先表達內心感受，刻意把握機遇，與人建立真實連結，這引發你思考：<b>當下社會強調成功，群交媒體盡是「完美生活」形象，容易讓個人產生「其他人生活都很好，只有我很孤單」的錯覺。在這孤獨與焦慮的世代，你是如何照顧你的心? </b>


<b>Your journey to the campsite & Dinner time:</b> No phone use 🚫📱

Reflection: Scrolling through phones causes the brain to continuously receive and process a large amount of trivial information, preventing true rest and leading to mental burnout. Frequent notifications blur the line between life and work, interrupt focus, and make it hard to live in the moment. This exercise aims to limit phone usage to help you reflect: <b>Between you and your phone, who is the owner? When the phone becomes a "yoke"—your heart constantly pulled, stimulated, and attracted by it—how will you face the "yoke" the phone places on your heart?</b>

<b>Your journey to the campsite & Dinner time:</b> Before speaking to anyone, you must first express your inner feelings.

Reflection: When was the last time you truly touched your heart and expressed your feelings? Often, our expressions carry many underlying feelings, sometimes multiple at once. Expressing inner feelings before speaking is a deliberate opportunity to build real connections with others. This prompts you to think: <b>Current society emphasizes success, and social media is full of "perfect life" images, easily creating the illusion that "everyone else's life is great, only I am lonely." In this generation of loneliness and anxiety, how do you take care of your heart?</b>
